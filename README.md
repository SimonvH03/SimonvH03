My role in the group is mainly DevOps/PO : I make sure my teammates have the tools they need, know what to work on, and keep making progress. I like seeing the bigger picture and helping others where possible.

Starting the curriculum with no prior experience I've found it works wonders to discuss ideas with others, however stupid or complicated they might seem.
